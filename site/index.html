03 The story

Written where the land runs out and the water begins.

Airon is Aaron Grieve — a songwriter from Cardigan (Aberteifi) on the west coast of Wales. The songs come from the same place the weather does: the estuary, the harbour, the long grey light of a Teifi morning.

Over one prolific year he's put out eleven singles, from the Welsh-language “Nos Da, Cariad” — picked up by BBC Introducing in Wales — to the brand-new “Munkee.” No band, no polish for polish's sake. Just voice, guitar, and whatever the room was feeling that night.

“Nos da, cariad.” — goodnight, love
